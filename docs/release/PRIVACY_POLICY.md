# Worth It privacy policy (draft)

_Draft for owner review; not yet published. Effective date: to be set at release._

Worth It is a browser extension that reviews your Walmart or Amazon shopping cart before checkout and suggests a few things worth a second thought. It never changes your cart, places orders, or connects to bank or retailer accounts.

## What Worth It reads

- **Cart pages (Walmart, Amazon):** the product name, item link or ID, quantity, and displayed prices of each cart line, while you are on the cart page.
- **Order pages, only if you turn on purchase recording:** item names, item IDs, quantities, prices, order numbers, order dates, and order status, only when you choose "Record these purchases."
- **Gmail, only if you connect it:** with your permission (read-only Gmail access), Worth It searches the date range you choose for likely order receipts, reads matching messages on your device to extract order details, and shows them to you before anything is saved.
- Worth It never reads checkout, payment, address, sign-in, or account fields.

## What Worth It stores

On your device only:
- purchase records you approve (from manual entry, Gmail import, or order pages), your answers to Worth It's questions, and feedback, in the extension's local database;
- your preferences, including allergies and dietary preferences you choose to enter.

Worth It does not keep email bodies, attachments, or Gmail sign-in tokens (Chrome manages the token). Nothing is synced to other devices or stored on Worth It servers.

## What leaves your device

- **Google (Gmail API):** search and message requests, only when you import from Gmail.
- **Worth It wording service (OpenAI):** to phrase the checkout recap, Worth It sends only the kinds of findings (for example "store-brand swap"), the names of its checks, and placeholder sentence templates. It never sends product names, prices, purchase history, email content, Gmail tokens, allergy or dietary settings, or financial information. If the service is unavailable, Worth It uses its own wording. [Owner: describe the proxy host, its retention (none of request bodies), and OpenAI's API data retention at release.]
- No analytics, advertising, tracking, or sale of data.

## Your controls

In Worth It's settings you can review, correct, search, export, and delete purchase records; remove all Gmail-imported or synthetic records; turn checkout prompts, purchase recording, and the investment illustration on or off; disconnect Gmail (and revoke access in your Google Account); and delete all Worth It data. Removing the extension deletes its local storage.

## Google API data

Worth It's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to find and extract order details you choose to import, is processed on your device, is not transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.

## Children

Worth It is not directed to children under 13.
