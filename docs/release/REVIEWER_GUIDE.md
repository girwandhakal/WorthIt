# Reviewer test instructions (draft)

No account with Worth It is needed. A Walmart or Amazon account is not required to see the cart review.

## Cart review

1. Install Worth It. Onboarding opens; choose **Finish setup**.
2. On walmart.com, add to the cart: Horizon Organic Vitamin D Whole Milk 64 fl oz, Stanley Quencher 30 oz tumbler, Squishmallows 5" plush, and Skittles Original Sharing Size. Open the cart.
3. A recap opens: "Before you check out, 3 things are worth a second thought." It cannot be skipped: use **Pause**, **Replay**, and **Captions** while it plays; **Continue** and **See full analysis** unlock when it ends. Open **Why?** on a highlight and **See full analysis**.
4. Press **Continue**. The panel closes; the cart is unchanged and checkout works normally. Worth It does not appear on checkout pages.
5. A cart with only Great Value milk, eggs, and rice shows only "Worth It · Reviewed 3 items".

## Purchase history and the question

1. Settings → **Purchase history** → **Add purchase**: "Stanley Quencher tumbler", dated 20 days ago, $24.97.
2. Reopen the cart from step 2 above: the tumbler now asks "Is this one replacing it, an extra, or for something different?" Choose **Replacing a worn one**; the advice switches to the store-brand tumbler.

## Optional Gmail import

Settings → **Import from Gmail** explains the read-only access first. Choose **Connect Gmail and find receipts**, approve Google's consent, and review the items before saving. **Disconnect Gmail** clears the token; imported records stay until removed in Purchase history.

## Optional purchase recording

Settings → turn on **Offer to record purchases**. On Walmart or Amazon order history, choose **Record these purchases** and review before saving. Payment, address, and account details are never read.
