## Worth It v0.1.0: Hacklanta 2026 hackathon release

**The last thing between your cart and your card: a moment to think twice.**

Worth It is a Chrome extension that reviews your Walmart or Amazon cart at checkout. It shows what's worth a second thought, what you'd keep, and what that money could become if invested. This is the version built for **Hacklanta 2026** (Fintech track).

### How it works
- **Shop as usual.** Nothing from Worth It appears on your cart page.
- **Click Checkout.** If there's something worth a second look, your first click is held once and a short full-screen presentation plays. It can't be skipped, and the controls unlock when it ends.
- **Decide.** It highlights at most three items, groups the rest, and shows your savings. Your next click goes straight through.

Worth It informs and suggests. It never edits your cart and never clicks anything for you.

### What it looks for
- **Store-brand swaps:** a cheaper store-brand equivalent, only when product data supports the comparison.
- **Recent similar purchases:** asks one question: replacing, extra, or a gift?
- **Novelty items:** no practical use, so "probably not worth it".
- **Food labels:** added sugar, processed meat, trans fat, and other fixed concerns, each with a vetted source. Silent when there's nothing to flag.
- **Investment illustration:** one hypothetical growth chart for the cart's total potential savings. On by default, easy to turn off.
- **See full analysis:** an on-request per-item breakdown with every check and source.

### Highlights in this release
- Walmart checkout flow, plus Amazon as a second retailer adapter.
- Full-screen animated presentation with product thumbnails, a running savings meter, and the growth chart.
- Rule-based assessment of the decision tree, with explainable results.
- Optional local purchase history: read-only Gmail receipt import that you review before anything is saved. A synthetic receipts script lets you try it without real emails.
- Fixes for Walmart's live cart layout, grouped cart rows, and sale (Rollback) prices.

### Privacy
- Everything runs on your device. No bank accounts or personal financial data.
- Gmail is optional and read-only. Receipts are parsed locally and email bodies are never kept.
- Product pages are read only for items already in your cart, never crawled.
- Optional wording polish sends placeholders only, with no names, prices, or history.
- No `tabs`, `history`, `cookies`, `webRequest`, `downloads`, or all-sites permissions.

### Install
Requires Node.js 22+ and Google Chrome.

```sh
git clone https://github.com/girwandhakal/WorthIt.git
cd WorthIt
git checkout v0.1.0
npm ci
npm run build
```

Then open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked**, and select `dist/chrome-mv3`. Open `walmart.com/cart` (or `amazon.com`) and click **Checkout**.

Gmail setup is in [docs/GMAIL_SETUP.md](../GMAIL_SETUP.md).

### Known limitations
- Loaded as an unpacked extension. It isn't published to the Chrome Web Store.
- Walmart is the primary flow. Amazon support is newer and less tested.
- Store-brand pairs and food-label judgments come from a reviewed reference set. Items outside it get no comment rather than a guess.
- The investment chart is hypothetical, not financial advice.
